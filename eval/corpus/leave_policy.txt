Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of annual leave per calendar year, prorated for anyone who joins partway through the year. Unused annual leave can be carried over into the next year, but no more than 5 days may be carried over, and carried-over days must be used within the first three months of the new year.

Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for any sick leave lasting more than three consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over to the next year and unused days are not paid out.

Parental Leave

New parents are eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental leave, available to either parent regardless of gender, and usable any time within the first year after the child's birth or adoption. Parental leave can be split into two separate blocks if needed.

Notice Periods

Employees who resign must give at least 4 weeks of written notice. During the first 90 days of employment, known as the probation period, the required notice period is reduced to 1 week for either party.

Probation Period

New employees are on a 90 day probation period during which performance and fit are formally reviewed. Either the employee or the company may end the employment relationship during probation with 1 week of notice.

Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require director-level sign-off and are reviewed every 6 months.
