Water and Boiling Point

Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius, which is 212 degrees Fahrenheit, at standard atmospheric pressure at sea level. At higher altitudes, where atmospheric pressure is lower, water boils at a lower temperature.

The Speed of Light

Light travels at approximately 299,792 kilometers per second in a vacuum. This is the maximum speed at which information or matter can travel according to the theory of relativity.

Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which plants convert sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide into glucose and oxygen. It takes place primarily in the chloroplasts of plant cells, using a pigment called chlorophyll to capture light energy.

Gravity

Gravity is the force that attracts two bodies with mass toward each other. On Earth's surface, objects accelerate toward the ground at approximately 9.8 meters per second squared, regardless of their mass, when air resistance is ignored.

DNA

DNA, or deoxyribonucleic acid, carries the genetic instructions used in the growth, development, and reproduction of all known living organisms. It is structured as a double helix made of two strands wound around each other.

The Human Heart

The average adult human heart beats around 60 to 100 times per minute at rest and pumps roughly 5 liters of blood through the body every minute.
